Straight answers

FAQ

What stops someone holding for an hour and dumping?

Nothing — and they should. Hours banked is balance multiplied by time, so one hour of holding earns one hour's worth. The wallet that held for forty hours simply outweighs them. There is no lockup and no penalty; the clock is the whole mechanism.

Do I need the person's permission to assign them supply?

No, and they do not need a wallet. Their share accrues to an address only they can claim, by posting a code from the account. If they never claim it, it stays there. AFK is not affiliated with any platform and coins are not endorsed by the people they are launched for.

How does someone claim?

They post their code from the handle it was launched on. The code is derived from the handle itself, so it can be checked by anyone without trusting us. Check a handle in the launch form to see exactly what theirs would be.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

Handles and avatars are read live from X. Volume and market cap come from Dexscreener, holders from the Robinhood Chain explorer. Nothing is cached overnight and nothing is estimated — where a number does not exist yet, the page says so instead of filling it in.

Is the token live?

Not yet. When it is, the contract address will appear at the top of this page and in the pinned post. Anyone posting a contract address before it appears here is scamming you.
